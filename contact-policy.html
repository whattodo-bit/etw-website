How We Handle Messages

Messages matter to us. This page explains what happens after you send one — how we read it, what we prioritise, and what you can realistically expect in return.

We Read Everything

Every message sent to ETW — whether through the contact form or by email — is read. We do not use automated filters to discard messages, and we do not delegate reading to a third party. If you took the time to write, we take the time to read it.

We Do Not Promise a Reply

Volume makes individual responses to every message impossible. If your message is something we can respond to meaningfully — a correction, a specific question, a serious collaboration enquiry — we will. If we cannot, your message still reaches us and still matters.

Two Ways to Contact Us

There are two ways to reach ETW. The contact form is anonymous and one-way — we receive your message but have no way to reply. Use it for suggestions, corrections, topic ideas, or anything you want to say without needing a response. Email is for messages where a reply matters — collaboration enquiries, copyright requests, or specific factual questions. If you need a response, email is the only channel that makes one possible.

What We Prioritise

Not all messages receive the same urgency. Factual corrections are handled first — accuracy is the foundation of everything we do. Legal and copyright matters follow. Collaboration enquiries are held on file and revisited when positions open. Suggestions and topic ideas are read and noted. General messages are read but rarely replied to individually.

What We Do Not Respond To

We do not reply to unsolicited advertising pitches, sponsored content requests, link exchange proposals, or messages sent in bad faith. These are discarded without response.

Response Time

There is no fixed response time. ETW is a small independent operation. Responses may take days or weeks depending on what is in progress. If something is urgent, say so clearly in your message. If you have not heard back after a reasonable period, one follow-up is welcome.

Your Privacy

If you use the contact form, our form provider may process basic technical data for spam prevention. ETW does not store or share this. If you contact by email, your details are held only for as long as needed to handle your enquiry. See the Legal page for full details.

We read everything. We may not always reply — but nothing sent here disappears.

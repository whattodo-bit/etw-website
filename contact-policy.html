How We Handle Messages

When you email us, we try to read every message with the time and importance it deserves. We consider each message and each suggestion valuable. We do not promise a reply — but if your message is something we can respond to meaningfully, we will.

If you want a response, use email. If you want to leave a suggestion or a note without expecting anything back, use the message form.

We do not see your identity when you send a message through the form. Messages are one-way and we cannot reply to them.

How We Handle Messages

When you email us, we read what comes in. We do not promise a reply. We respond when a message is relevant and time allows. Many messages will not receive a response, and that is not a reflection of their value.

When you send a message through the form, it reaches us one way. We do not see your email address or how you sent it. Use it for suggestions or notes you do not need a reply to.

We do not track you. We do not sell data. Messages are not encrypted end to end, so do not send anything sensitive.

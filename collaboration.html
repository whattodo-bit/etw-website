Work With Us

ETW is an independent project open to select collaboration. Every collaborator works to one standard: the content comes first.

Creative

We are looking for people who hold themselves to a high standard and care about the craft — not people filling a role, but people who want to shape how ETW looks and sounds. Roles include video editors, motion designers, illustrators, and narrators. We expect attention to detail, the ability to work with research-heavy material, a willingness to revise, and the discipline to maintain consistency across a growing body of work. If you have examples that demonstrate these qualities, we want to see them. To express interest, write to collaborate@explainingtheworld.com.

Research

ETW's foundation is accuracy. Research collaborators work with primary sources, cross-check claims independently, and flag anything uncertain rather than assume. Roles include historians, academic researchers, translators working with source material in other languages, and fact-checkers. We expect familiarity with historical research methods, the ability to distinguish primary from secondary sources, and a willingness to go deep rather than fast. Academic background is valued but not required — demonstrated ability matters more. To express interest, write to collaborate@explainingtheworld.com.

Brand Partnerships

ETW is open to partnerships that respect editorial independence. We do not accept arrangements that influence content, introduce bias toward any figure, nation, institution, or product, or compromise the integrity of our research. Partnerships must be transparent, non-editorial, and aligned with our mission of producing accurate, in-depth educational content. We do not run traditional advertising. If you represent a brand or organisation that shares these values, reach us at partnerships@explainingtheworld.com.

How to Apply

Send your expression of interest by email. Include which area you are applying for (Creative, Research, or Brand), a short description of your background and relevant experience, examples of your work or a portfolio link, and what specifically draws you to ETW. We read every submission. We aim to review all submissions within four to six weeks. If you have not heard back after that period, one follow-up is welcome.

For creative and research roles: collaborate@explainingtheworld.com
For brand and partnership enquiries: partnerships@explainingtheworld.com

The Process

01 — Submit
Send your expression of interest with the information listed above. Include only what is relevant — we read everything but value specificity over length.

02 — Review
We review all submissions against current and upcoming needs. We retain all expressions of interest on file and reach out when the right opportunity arises.

03 — Agreement and Brief
Before any work begins, we issue a contributor agreement covering scope, compensation, IP ownership, and confidentiality. No work starts without a signed agreement in place.

Contributor Terms

All collaborators are required to agree to a contributor agreement prior to beginning work. This covers intellectual property ownership, confidentiality of unpublished material, editorial standards, and compensation terms, discussed individually during onboarding. Work produced in collaboration with ETW — unless explicitly agreed otherwise in writing — remains the intellectual property of Explaining The World upon delivery and payment. Depending on the nature of the engagement, collaborators may be required to sign a non-disclosure agreement before receiving access to unpublished scripts, research notes, or production materials.

Compensation

ETW compensates collaborators for their work. Rates and structures depend on the nature, scope, and duration of the engagement and are agreed individually before any work begins. We do not ask for unpaid contributions. Speculative or trial work is not requested without compensation.

Moral Rights

Contributors retain the right to be credited for their work where agreed in writing. ETW retains the right to edit, adapt, and present contributed work as part of its editorial output. Specific credit arrangements are agreed individually before work begins.

Revision and Rejection

ETW reserves the right to request revisions or reject submitted work that does not meet the agreed brief or editorial standard. Compensation terms in cases of rejection after completion are agreed individually prior to engagement and stated in the contributor agreement.

Exclusivity

Collaborators are asked not to produce substantially similar content on the same specific topic for a competing platform within thirty days of delivery to ETW. This does not restrict general work in the same subject area.

Dispute Resolution

Any disputes arising from collaboration with ETW will be handled under applicable law. The jurisdiction is agreed individually with collaborators based on their location at the time of engagement.

Data and Privacy

When you submit an expression of interest, your contact details and application materials are held on file for the purpose of reviewing your submission and following up when relevant opportunities arise. This data is not shared with third parties. If you would like your details removed from our records at any time, contact us through the contact form and we will action it promptly. If you are based in the UK or EU, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of any personal data we hold about you.

No Speculative Work

No work should be started, produced, or submitted prior to a signed contributor agreement. ETW does not accept or review unsolicited completed work and takes no responsibility for work produced without a prior written agreement in place.

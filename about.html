About ETW

Explaining The World is an independent media brand built around one idea: understanding power — how it is taken, held, and lost.

Explaining The World is an independent media brand operating across history and science. We produce long-form content for a global English-speaking audience — analytical, rigorously researched, and built on the belief that understanding the world requires understanding what came before it. ETW carries no political alignment, no institutional backing, and no editorial obligation to anyone except the subject matter.

Most people consume news and events without the context that explains them. Headlines describe what happened. ETW is interested in why it happened, and what it reveals about how power, technology, and human nature actually operate over time. That context is not optional. It is the point. We exist because the gap between event and explanation has become one of the defining problems of the information environment.

The History channel is live. We are publishing in-depth series on the decisions that shaped empires, wars, and revolutions — the choices of rulers, generals, and strategists who altered the course of events, examined on their own terms. The Science channel is in development, bringing the same analytical lens to physics, biology, and the systems that govern the physical world. Both channels operate under one principle: depth over speed.

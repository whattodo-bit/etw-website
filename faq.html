Questions

What is Explaining The World?

An independent project producing researched content for a global English-speaking audience — currently focused on history, with work in progress on other subjects.

Who is behind it?

ETW is an independent project. No corporate structure. No sponsors. One standard — get it right.

What topics do you cover?

History first — empires, leaders, and the mechanics of power. Other subjects will follow as the project grows.

How is the content researched?

We go into the depth of every source and every book to find every detail that matters. This takes time. Nothing is brushed past.

How can I collaborate or get in touch?

See the Collaboration and Contact sections on the About page. Genuine, relevant messages are always welcome.

Why are ETW videos not like other established channels?

Production improves with time and resources. The priority has always been the content — the research, the accuracy, the depth. A well-researched video with average production beats a visually polished video with nothing to say. Content quality comes first. Everything else follows.

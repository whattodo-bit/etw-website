What is Explaining The World?

An independent project producing researched content for a global English-speaking audience — currently focused on history, expanding into other subjects with time.

Who is behind it?

One person, for now — reading, researching, writing, and producing every piece. This is a project, not an organisation.

What topics do you cover?

History first — empires, leaders, and the mechanics of power. Other subjects will follow as the project grows.

How is the content researched?

We go into the depth of every source and every book to find every detail that matters. This takes time. Nothing is brushed past.

How can I collaborate or get in touch?

See the Collaboration and Contact sections on the About page. Genuine, relevant messages are always welcome.

Why is the video quality not perfect?

Production quality improves with time and resources. The priority has always been the content — the research, the accuracy, the depth of analysis. A well-researched video with average production beats a visually perfect video with nothing to say. Quality of content comes first. Everything else follows.

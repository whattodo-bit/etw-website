Work With Us

ETW is an independent project open to select collaboration. Positions are not always open. If nothing is currently listed as active, you are welcome to register your interest for the future — we keep a record and reach out when the right opportunity arises. Every collaborator works to one standard: the content comes first.

Creative

We are looking for people who hold themselves to a high standard and care about the craft — not people filling a role, but people who want to shape how ETW looks and sounds. Roles include video editors, motion designers, illustrators, and narrators. We expect attention to detail, the ability to work with research-heavy material, a willingness to revise, and the discipline to maintain consistency across a growing body of work. If you have examples that demonstrate these qualities, we want to see them.

Research

ETW's foundation is accuracy. Research collaborators work with primary sources, cross-check claims independently, and flag anything uncertain rather than assume. Roles include historians, academic researchers, translators working with source material in other languages, and fact-checkers. We expect familiarity with historical research methods, the ability to distinguish primary from secondary sources, and a willingness to go deep rather than fast. Academic background is valued but not required — demonstrated ability matters more.

Brand Partnerships

ETW is open to partnerships that respect editorial independence. We do not accept arrangements that influence content, introduce bias toward any figure, nation, institution, or product, or compromise the integrity of our research. Partnerships must be transparent, non-editorial, and aligned with our mission of producing accurate, in-depth educational content. We do not run traditional advertising. If you represent a brand or organisation that shares these values, we are open to a conversation.

How to Apply

Send your expression of interest through the contact form. Include which area you are applying for (Creative, Research, or Brand), a short description of your background and relevant experience, examples of your work or a portfolio link, and what specifically draws you to ETW. We read every submission. Due to volume, we are not always able to respond individually, but we retain all expressions of interest on file and follow up when positions become available.

Contributor Terms

All collaborators are required to agree to a contributor agreement prior to beginning work. This covers intellectual property ownership, confidentiality of unpublished material, editorial standards, and compensation terms, discussed individually during onboarding. Work produced in collaboration with ETW — unless explicitly agreed otherwise in writing — remains the intellectual property of Explaining The World upon delivery and payment.

Compensation

ETW compensates collaborators for their work. Rates and structures depend on the nature, scope, and duration of the engagement and are agreed individually before any work begins. We do not ask for unpaid contributions. Speculative or trial work is not requested without compensation.

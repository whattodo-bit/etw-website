Work With Us

Collaboration

ETW is open to research collaboration, academic partnerships, and select creative projects.

01 — Research & Academic

Research & Academic

ETW cites its sources rigorously and takes accuracy as a non-negotiable standard. We are open to subject matter experts who wish to contribute to scripts, provide fact-checking on specific historical periods, or collaborate on research-led content. If you have domain expertise in history, geopolitics, or a related field and believe your knowledge could strengthen our work, we want to hear from you.

02 — Creative & Production

Creative & Production

ETW has a defined aesthetic — dark, serious, cinematic. We are open to motion designers, voice artists, composers, and visual artists who align with that sensibility and can bring discipline and craft to a long-form production environment. Speculative or portfolio-based approaches are welcome. We evaluate on the quality of the work, not the size of the résumé.

03 — Brand & Sponsorship

Brand & Sponsorship

ETW is not currently accepting sponsors. When we do, it will be disclosed fully and limited to brands that do not conflict with editorial independence. We will not accept sponsorship from political organisations, advocacy groups, or any entity seeking to influence editorial content. Independence is not negotiable.

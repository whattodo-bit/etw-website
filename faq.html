Questions

What is Explaining The World?+

An independent project producing researched content for a global English-speaking audience — focused on history, with work in progress on other subjects and languages.

Who is behind it?+

ETW is an independent project. No corporate structure. No sponsors. One standard — quality.

What topics do you cover?+

History — empires, leaders, and the mechanics of power. Other subjects will follow as the project grows.

How is ETW content researched?+

Each topic begins with research across multiple sources. Claims are cross-checked against other references before anything is read in depth. Important points are extracted, turned into notes, and verified again before publishing. Mistakes can still happen — when they do, we take full responsibility and correct them.

How often do you publish?+

It depends on the topic. A focused video on a single book or event takes less time than a broad historical analysis. There is no fixed schedule — each video takes as long as the research demands.

How can I collaborate or get in touch?+

See the Collaboration and Contact sections in the About page.

Why are ETW videos not like other established channels?+

Production improves with time and resources. The priority has always been the content — the research, the accuracy, the depth. A well-researched video with average production beats a visually polished video with nothing to say. Content quality comes first. Everything else follows.

Do you accept sponsorships?+

No. ETW stays free and independent. We accept donations from viewers who want to support the work, but not sponsorships. The only exception would be neutral, non-editorial sponsors — never anything that shapes or influences the content itself. The content stays independent. Always.
